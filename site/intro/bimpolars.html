bimpolars: polars dataframes for BIM-data

bimpolars reads BIM-data into polars dataframes from IFC or Speckle (so practically any BIM-software, see Speckle's integrations).

Georeference the BIM-data in a way GIS understands, such that you can bring 3D geospatial context into BIM or BIM-data into GIS:
