specklestuff: Speckle models with 3D geospatial context, in the browser and in Python notebooks

specklestuff runs Speckle's web viewer in the browser and in Python notebooks (marimo, Jupyter), fed with Speckle objects or bimpolars dataframes, no Speckle server needed.

Its GeoSpeckleViewer puts georeferenced BIM-data in its 3D geospatial context, on the map among its neighbours:
